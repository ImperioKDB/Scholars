# Scholars Investment Memorandum: Fact-Check and Assumption Register

Prepared for the revision of the friends-and-family investment memorandum.

## Findings to use in the investor version

### Market size

The strongest defensible market reference found is the National Universities Commission's 2019 Nigerian University System Statistical Digest. It reports **2,159,461 students at all levels in Nigerian universities in 2019**, including **785,259 new undergraduate entrants** that year. This is university enrolment only, not all Nigerian tertiary students, and it is historical rather than a current 2026 estimate. The memorandum should use it as context, not as a current total-addressable-market forecast.

Recommended wording:

> Nigeria's university system alone recorded 2.16 million students at all levels in the NUC's 2019 statistical digest, with 785,259 new undergraduate entrants that year. Scholars is initially targeting a small, measurable segment of this broader population rather than claiming to serve the whole market.

Source: [1]

The NBS Education Statistics publication explains that university statistics are collated through the NUC and presents education-statistics methodology, but it does not provide a clean current national tertiary-total figure in the extracted sections reviewed. Do not combine NBS, NUC, UNESCO, and third-party figures as if they measure the same population.

### CAC registration costs

The official CAC fee schedule shows that incorporation of a private company limited by shares with issued share capital of ₦1 million or less is listed at **₦10,000** for the applicable private-company categories, while name reservation is listed at **₦500**. These are official filing fees only. They do not cover legal drafting, tax work, professional fees, certificates, bank setup, or other transaction costs.

The memorandum's ₦50,000 CAC line can remain as a provisional budget, but it should be labelled **official filing fees plus a contingency allowance**, not presented as a quoted all-in incorporation cost.

Source: [2]

### Data protection

The NDPC's updated guidance states that an organisation processing personal data of more than **200 data subjects in six months**, or operating in listed sectors including education, can fall within the Commission's designation of controllers or processors of major importance. The guidance also describes higher thresholds and categories, including more than 1,000 and more than 5,000 data subjects in six months, and lists registration fees by category.

Scholars already processes sensitive student-related information, including academic and eligibility data. The memorandum should therefore say that privacy compliance is an operating requirement to confirm with Nigerian counsel, rather than treating a ₦50,000 compliance line as a guaranteed fee. The exact category and obligations should be confirmed with a qualified Nigerian data-protection professional.

Source: [3]

### Paid acquisition assumptions

Meta's official pricing page explains that advertising cost varies according to factors such as auction conditions, audience, objective, placement, and budget. The source does not support a fixed Nigeria-specific cost-per-signup benchmark.

The memorandum should remove the statement that paid signups in Nigeria may cost ₦300–₦800 each unless Scholars has its own measured campaign data. Replace it with a test plan:

- Start with a small tranche.
- Define a signup, completed profile, and activated student separately.
- Track cost per event by channel and campaign.
- Scale only after a cohort shows acceptable activation and retention.

Source: [4]

## Claims that still require founder confirmation

- Current registered users: 216.
- Current catalogue size: 60+ hand-verified scholarships and opportunities.
- Number of users who completed profiles.
- Weekly and monthly active users.
- Saves, applications, deadline reminders, and return visits per active user.
- Students who applied to an opportunity discovered through Scholars.
- Number and wording of student testimonials.
- CAC incorporation and legal quotes.
- Any competitor names or market-share claims.
- Whether the founder story includes financial hardship, a single-parent household, working to fund UTME, or receiving a scholarship. Those points should be included only with the founder's approval and in the founder's own words.

## Claims to remove or qualify

- Fixed paid-acquisition benchmarks without campaign evidence.
- A current national tertiary-market total derived from old or mismatched sources.
- Any implication that CAC registration, NDPC compliance, legal documents, and trademark work will definitely fit the current budget lines.
- Any implied revenue forecast. The business model remains unproven.
- Any claim that the automated discovery tool is production-ready if it is not running in production.
