# Scholars Investor Evidence Appendix

This appendix should accompany the memorandum once the founder supplies or approves the evidence. Do not use invented screenshots, metrics, or testimonials.

## Product screenshots

Capture current production screens at a readable mobile and desktop size:

1. **Homepage:** shows the product proposition and primary path into discovery.
2. **Profile and matching:** shows a student profile and an explained match result. Redact names, email addresses, scores, or other personal details unless the person has given permission.
3. **Dashboard:** shows matches, saved opportunities, deadlines, or application tracking using a demo or consented account.
4. **Opportunity detail:** shows verification context, requirements, deadline, and the next action.
5. **Community:** shows a discussion and the privacy-safe contributor card. Do not show emails or anonymous author identity.

## Testimonials

Add three to five approved verbatim comments. For each testimonial, record:

- the exact quote;
- whether the student gave permission to use it in investor materials;
- the student's preferred attribution, such as first name and institution or anonymous student;
- the date and context of the feedback; and
- whether the quote has been lightly edited for spelling only.

Suggested evidence request:

> What did Scholars help you do that was difficult before? Did it save time, help you find a relevant opportunity, or help you track an application?

Do not turn a general compliment into a claim about receiving funding unless the student explicitly confirms that outcome.

## Metrics snapshot

Before every investor conversation, refresh:

- registered users;
- users with completed profiles;
- weekly active users;
- monthly active users;
- first match viewed;
- saved opportunities;
- applications started;
- applications submitted, if measurable;
- returning-user rate;
- verified listings and listing freshness; and
- acquisition source.

Label each number with its date and define the metric in plain language.

## Founder story approval

The founder may add a more personal account of financial hardship, family circumstances, work undertaken to fund education, or receiving a scholarship. Include only details the founder is comfortable disclosing and can substantiate. Do not use private family information as marketing copy without explicit approval.
