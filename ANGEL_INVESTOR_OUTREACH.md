## Personalisation checklist

Before sending, replace the bracketed fields and add one genuine reason for contacting that person. Mention a relevant investment, education, technology, university, or founder interest only if it is true. Use the latest user and catalogue counts from the dashboard. Attach the one-page summary only after checking that the figures are current.

Do not claim that the company has revenue, product-market fit, a guaranteed market, a guaranteed return, or a fixed acquisition cost. Do not describe the round as a public offer. If an investor asks about valuation, equity, a SAFE-style instrument, or legal rights, say that the structure is still being finalised with professional legal advice.

## Suggested outreach sequence

Start with 10–15 highly relevant people rather than sending the message widely. Track the date, channel, personalisation, response, questions, meeting, and next step. Use the first conversations to improve the pitch before contacting the next group.
