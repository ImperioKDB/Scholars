# Scholars: One-Page Investor Summary

## The problem

Nigerian students discover scholarships and educational opportunities across scattered websites, WhatsApp groups, social posts, and PDFs. Requirements differ, deadlines move, and outdated listings reduce trust. A long list does not tell a student which opportunities are worth pursuing.

## The product

Scholars is a student-first discovery and matching platform. A student creates one profile. Scholars compares that profile against structured opportunity requirements, explains the result, and helps the student save, track, and act on opportunities.

The live product includes eligibility-based matching, explained scores, a catalogue, deadline visibility, application tracking, AI-assisted drafting with student review, document checklists, community discussions, achievements, and an installable mobile web experience.

## Evidence so far

- 216 registered users.
- 60+ hand-verified scholarships and opportunities.
- ₦0 spent on paid marketing to date.
- Live product built end to end by the founder.
- Current revenue: ₦0.

## Why now

The National Universities Commission recorded 2.16 million students at all levels in Nigerian universities in its 2019 statistical digest, including 785,259 new undergraduate entrants that year. Scholars is beginning with a focused segment of this wider student population, not claiming the whole market. [1]

## Business model to test

Students remain free while the audience is built. The first commercial tests are provider tools, qualified applicant reach, privacy-safe aggregate insights, and partnerships. Providers will not be able to buy better match rankings.

## Round

Scholars is considering a ₦1,500,000 friends-and-family round, with a ₦1,000,000 minimum close if necessary. Funds would support formalisation, staged acquisition experiments, a campus ambassador pilot, infrastructure, partnerships, and contingency. Spending is released against activation and retention evidence.

## First 90-day outcomes

The company will complete formalisation, establish acquisition and activation measurement, test two or three channels, pilot ambassadors, grow the user base toward 1,000, maintain verification quality, and begin provider conversations.

## Risks

This is a pre-revenue, single-founder company with an unproven business model. Paid acquisition economics and provider willingness to pay are not known. Verification is still manual. Student data requires careful privacy and security controls. Investors should only invest money they can afford to lose entirely.

## Vision

Scholars starts with scholarship matching and aims to become opportunity infrastructure connecting students, providers, universities, fellowships, internships, competitions, and other educational opportunities across Africa.
