## Student summary

YouthSync invites middle and high school students worldwide to submit an original English essay of no more than 500 words on how artificial intelligence may affect their lives and the people around them. There is no entry fee.

The form requests the student’s full name, email address, high school, current grade, city/country, essay title, and essay text. It includes a required publication-permission question stating that selected students agree YouthSync may contact them about featuring their submission on its website or social media. Younger applicants should review this condition with a parent or guardian. Students should not provide passwords, bank details, payment, or government ID.
