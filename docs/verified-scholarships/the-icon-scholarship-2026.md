## Student summary

The ICON Scholarship provides tuition support for newly admitted Nigerian university students from all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. It is aimed at students who have recently gained admission after the UTME/JAMB process. The application is free.

Applicants should prepare a valid means of identification, university admission letter, WAEC/NECO result, JAMB/UTME result, birth certificate, and cover letter. The award amount and payment method are not published, so students should confirm those details with White Avenue Group if the application form changes. No application or processing fee should be requested.
